[hubverse](https://hubverse.io/#improving-public-health-outcomes-through-collaborative-modeling)

The **hubverse** is an open-source suite of data standards and software for building and running collaborative infectious disease modeling hubs. Its goal is to improve public health outcomes. Hub administrators use configuration files based on the hubverse schema to set structured guidelines for tabular model output submissions, which can then be easily validated, ensembled, and visualized. The software is written in R, Python, and JavaScript and supports every phase of a hub's life cycle: setup and management (hubAdmin), submission validation (hubValidations), data access (hubData), ensembling (hubEnsembles), evaluation (hubEvals), and visualization (hubVis). The hubverse is used by organizations including the US CDC, ECDC, the California Department of Public Health, Johns Hopkins, and the Reich Lab. It helps modelers worldwide share insights in real time and inform critical decisions with robust, validated forecasts.
