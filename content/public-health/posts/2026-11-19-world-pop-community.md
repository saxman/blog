[Join the Conversation.](https://community.worldpop.org/)

The WorldPop Community is a Discourse-based discussion forum for users of WorldPop's open geospatial population datasets and tools. It is organized into categories for announcements, learning events, helpdesk support, data and tools discussions, and real-world applications. It gives researchers and practitioners a shared place to troubleshoot, exchange knowledge, and apply population data in areas such as public health and development.
