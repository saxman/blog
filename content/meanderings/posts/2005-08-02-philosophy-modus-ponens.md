[Modus ponens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modus_ponens): "In propositional logic, modus ponens, also known as modus ponendo ponens, conditional elimination, implication elimination, or affirming the antecedent, is a deductive argument form and rule of inference. It can be summarized as "P implies Q. P is true. Therefore, Q must also be true.""
