[Amatonormativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amatonormativity)

Colloquially known as "Crush Culture".

Amatonormativity is the set of societal assumptions that everyone benefits from, and is seeking, an exclusive, long-term romantic relationship. Philosopher Elizabeth Brake coined the term in her 2012 book *Minimizing Marriage*, modeling it on heteronormativity and deriving it from the Latin *amatus* ("loved"). The concept describes pressures that stigmatize single, asexual, and aromantic people and privilege romantic relationships over friendships, including in law and marriage. The term has since been used in queer theory, literary studies, and self-help and popular books on aromanticism.
