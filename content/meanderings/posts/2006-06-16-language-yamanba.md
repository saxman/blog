Yamanba is an extreme style of ganguro fashion.

[Ganguro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ganguro): "Ganguro (ガングロ) is an alternative fashion trend among young Japanese women which peaked in popularity around the year 2000 and evolved from gyaru."
