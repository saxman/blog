[Mise en abyme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mise_en_abyme): "In Western art history, mise en abyme is the technique of placing a copy of an image within itself, often in a way that suggests an infinitely recurring sequence. In film theory and literary theory, it refers to the story within a story technique."
