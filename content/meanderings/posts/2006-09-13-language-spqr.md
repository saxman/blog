[SPQR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPQR): "SPQR or S.P.Q.R., an initialism for Senatus Populusque Romanus, is an emblematic phrase referring to the government of the Roman Republic. It appears on documents made public by an inscription in stone or metal, in dedications of monuments and public works, and on some Roman currency."
