[Bésame Mucho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C3%A9same_Mucho): ""Bésame Mucho" is a bolero song written in 1932 by Mexican songwriter Consuelo Velázquez. One of the most important songs in the history of Latin music, it became one of the most popular pop standards of the 20th century as well as a jazz standard."
