[Way of St. James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Way_of_St._James): The Way of St. James or St. James' Way is the pilgrimage route to the Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela in Galicia in northwestern Spain, where tradition has it that the remains of the apostle Saint James are buried.
