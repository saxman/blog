[Unit 731](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_731): "Unit 731 was a secret biological and chemical warfare research unit run by the Imperial Japanese Army between 1933 and 1945. Officially known as the Manchu Detachment 731, it was also referred to as the Kamo Detachment and the Ishii Unit."
