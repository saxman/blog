[Kunoichi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunoichi): "Kunoichi is a Japanese term for "woman". In popular culture, it is often used for female ninja or practitioners of ninjutsu (ninpo)."

くノ一; くの一 【くのいち】 (n) kunoichi: female ninja.
