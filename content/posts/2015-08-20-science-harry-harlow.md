[Harry Harlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Harlow#Monkey_studies): "Harry Frederick Harlow (October 31, 1905 – December 6, 1981) was an American psychologist best known for his maternal-separation, dependency needs, and social isolation experiments on rhesus monkeys, which manifested the importance of caregiving and companionship in social and cognitive development."
