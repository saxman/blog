[Wabi-sabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wabi-sabi): "In traditional Japanese aesthetics, wabi-sabi (侘び寂び) centers on the acceptance of transience and imperfection. It is often described as the appreciation of beauty that is "imperfect, impermanent, and incomplete". It is prevalent in many forms of Japanese art."

詫び寂び 【わびさび】 (n) wabisabi: humble simplicity.
