[Damnatio memoriae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damnatio_memoriae): "Damnatio memoriae is a modern Latin phrase meaning "condemnation of memory" or "damnation of memory", indicating that a person is to be excluded from official accounts, or remembered after death in a way contrary to what that person may have desired."
