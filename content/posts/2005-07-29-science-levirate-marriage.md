[Levirate marriage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levirate_marriage): "Levirate marriage is a type of marriage in which the brother of a deceased man is obliged to marry his widowed sister-in-law. Levirate marriage has been practiced by societies where exogamous marriage is forbidden."
