[Geta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geta_(footwear)): "Geta are traditional Japanese footwear resembling flip-flops. A kind of sandal, geta have a flat wooden base held on the foot with a fabric thong, and are elevated with up to three "teeth," which keeps the foot raised above the ground."

下駄 【げた】 (n) geta: wooden clogs.
