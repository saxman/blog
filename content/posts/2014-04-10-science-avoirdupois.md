[Avoirdupois](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avoirdupois): "The avoirdupois system is a system of [weights](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Units_of_measurement) (more properly, [mass](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass)) based on a [pound](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pound_(mass)) of 16 [ounces](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ounce). It is the everyday system of weight used in the [United States](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and is still used to varying degrees in everyday life in the [United Kingdom](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom), [Canada](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), and some other former British colonies despite the official [adoption](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrication) of the [metric system](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_system)."
