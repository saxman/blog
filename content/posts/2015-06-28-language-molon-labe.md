[Molon labe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molon_labe): "Molon labe (Greek: μολὼν λαβέ), meaning "come and get it", is a classical expression of defiance. When the Persian armies demanded that the Greeks surrender their weapons at the Battle of Thermopylae, King Leonidas I responded with this phrase. It is an exemplary use of a laconic phrase."['](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/pengoopmcjnbflcjbmoeodbmoflcgjlk)
