[Natural Amenities Drive Rural Population Change](https://www.ers.usda.gov/publications/41048)

*Natural Amenities Drive Rural Population Change* is a 1999 report by David McGranahan, published by the USDA Economic Research Service. It develops a natural amenities index based on climate, topography, and water area, and finds that the index is strongly related to nonmetropolitan county population change from 1970 to 1996. Average population growth was 1 percent in counties scoring low on the index and 120 percent in counties scoring high. The report also links amenities to employment change, retirement and recreation counties, and regional differences in which amenities attract residents.
