[Yoshiko Kawashima](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawashima_Yoshiko): "Yoshiko Kawashima (川島 芳子 Kawashima Yoshiko?, 24 May 1907 – 25 March 1948) was a Manchu princess brought up in Japan, who served as a spy in the service of the Japanese Kwantung Army and Manchukuo during the Second World War."
