[Hypernymy and hyponymy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypernymy_and_hyponymy): "Hypernymy and hyponymy are the semantic relations between a generic term (hypernym) and a more specific term (hyponym). The hypernym is also called a supertype, umbrella term, or blanket term. The hyponym names a subtype of the hypernym."
