[Sator Square](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sator_Square): "The Sator Square (or Rotas Square) is a word square containing a Latin palindrome:

SATOR

AREPO

TENET

OPERA

ROTAS

The five words may be read top-to-bottom, bottom-to-top, left-to-right, or right-to-left."
