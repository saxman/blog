[Human Context Protocol - The Standardized Protocol for Human-Computer Interaction](https://humancontextprotocol.com/)

Human Context Protocol (HCP) is a protocol that lets users store their own personal context, such as preferences, dietary needs and payment tokens, and share task-scoped portions of it with AI agents and applications through a server API, while keeping sensitive data like contacts, addresses and bank accounts private. It matters because it moves control of personal data from large platforms to individuals, so many kinds of services can be personalized without each building its own user model or storing sensitive customer data.
