[Asta: Advancing Scientific AI with Agents & Benchmarks](https://allenai.org/asta)

Asta is an agentic ecosystem from Ai2 that advances scientific discovery. It was co-designed with domain scientists to inspire new research methods while preserving user agency and scientific freedom. It offers Asta agents, which are AI research assistants that help scientists frame research questions, trace ideas to evidence, and see what is established or unresolved in a field. AstaBench supplies real-world benchmarks and leaderboards for evaluating and comparing AI agents. Developers can also use Asta resources to build production-ready agents. Recent highlights include work with Providence Swedish Cancer Institute (PARC), where AutoDiscovery helped reveal and validate an immune signal in invasive lobular breast cancer. Another is the open-source release of AstaBrief, an 8B open-weights model for generating cited scientific reports. Users can also sign up for Asta Preview to try new capabilities.
