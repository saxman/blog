[Claude-shaped science](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-shaped-science)

A guest post by physicist Matthew Schwartz describing BootLoops, an open-source harness and toolkit he built with Claude to tackle "Claude-shaped" problems: exact quantitative calculations where techniques from physics, math, or computer science can be transferred to other fields. It recounts results ranging from new elliptic Feynman integrals to findings in ecology, population genetics, economics, and linguistics, made with domain experts. It also offers practical lessons on the strengths, failure modes, and appropriate use of agentic AI in scientific research.
