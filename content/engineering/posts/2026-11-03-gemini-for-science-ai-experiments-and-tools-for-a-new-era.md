[Gemini for Science: AI experiments and tools for a new era of discovery](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/gemini-for-science-io-2026/)

Gemini for Science, announced by Google at I/O 2026, is a collection of AI tools and experiments built to speed up core steps of the scientific method. It includes three experimental prototypes on Google Labs. **Hypothesis Generation** is built with Co-Scientist and uses a multi-agent "idea tournament" to produce hypotheses backed by citations. **Computational Discovery** is built with AlphaEvolve and ERA and generates and scores thousands of code variations in parallel. **Literature Insights** is built with NotebookLM and structures and synthesizes scientific literature. Access is opening gradually at labs.google/science. The program also includes **Science Skills** for Google Antigravity, a bundle that integrates more than 30 life science databases and tools such as UniProt, the AlphaFold Database and the AlphaGenome API. Enterprise versions are offered through Google Cloud and are already used in private preview by BASF, Klarna, Daiichi Sankyo, Bayer Crop Science and the U.S. National Labs. Validation comes from more than 100 partner institutions, a trusted tester community, conference pilots for agentic peer review, and ERA and Co-Scientist papers published in Nature.
