[How American Universities Are Using AI Models From Meta to Accelerate Medical and Scientific Breakthroughs](https://research.meta.ai/blog/supporting-medical-scientific-research)

Meta is launching a university research support program with five US institutions: Caltech, The Ohio State University College of Medicine, Louisiana State University, Indiana University, and UTHealth Houston. Support varies by project and includes access to Meta AI models, direct collaboration with Meta researchers, and funding for select projects. The models include the Muse family (Spark Open, Wonder, Spark, and Glimmer), SAM, V-JEPA, DINO, and SeamlessM4T, and some projects also use Meta wearables. Caltech will test whether frontier models can do original research in math, physics, chemistry, biology, and computer science, such as formulating and proving conjectures, predicting novel outcomes, and producing verifiable artifacts. The medical projects cover early heart failure risk prediction, chest X-ray report drafting, Alzheimer's diagnosis agents, neurosurgical registries, infant tracheostomy monitoring, VR medical training, maternal and newborn care in low-resource settings, dementia-aware conversational AI, and glioblastoma clinical agents. Faculty designed their own studies, and healthcare projects run models within university environments, so patient data is never shared with Meta.
