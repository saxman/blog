[walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering: Harness engineering beginner tutorial, from 0 to 1](https://github.com/walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering)

Learn Harness Engineering is an open, project-based course on building the environment around AI coding agents: instructions, state, verification, scope, and session lifecycle. It argues that reliability comes from the harness rather than the model alone. It pairs lectures with hands-on projects built on an Electron knowledge-base app and supplies ready-to-use templates. Recent additions cover loop engineering, graph engineering, and breakdowns of production harnesses such as Claude Code and Codex.
