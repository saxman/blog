[AI agents enter the ‘wet lab’](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03270-1)

This *Nature* News & Views article, "AI agents enter the 'wet lab'" by James Zou (published 4 November 2026), comments on a *Nature* paper by Wu *et al.* that introduces AutoDNA, a laboratory in which AI agents plan and carry out molecular-biology experiments involving DNA with relatively little human intervention. The piece notes that AI has mostly been used in science for computational tasks such as data analysis, prediction and literature searching. Hands-on 'wet lab' work, by contrast, still demands weeks or months of human effort to test AI-designed molecules. The author argues that AutoDNA shows how AI systems can begin to close the gap between proposing experiments and performing them.
