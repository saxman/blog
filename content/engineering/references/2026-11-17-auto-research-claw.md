[aiming-lab/AutoResearchClaw: Fully autonomous & self-evolving research from idea to paper. Chat an Idea. Get a Paper. 🦞](https://github.com/aiming-lab/AutoResearchClaw)

AutoResearchClaw is an open-source, 23-stage pipeline that turns a research topic into a full academic paper. It covers literature discovery from real sources, sandboxed experiments, multi-agent review, citation verification and LaTeX export. It can run fully autonomously or with human-in-the-loop co-pilot modes, and it adds domain-specific experiment agents and the ARC-Bench benchmark for evaluating autonomous research systems.
