[harbor-framework/harbor: Framework for evaluating and improving agents](https://github.com/harbor-framework/harbor)

Harbor is an open-source framework from the creators of Terminal-Bench for evaluating and optimizing AI agents and language models in containerized environments. It runs agents such as Claude Code, OpenHands, and Codex CLI against benchmarks locally or in parallel on cloud sandbox providers, and it can generate rollouts for reinforcement learning. As the official harness for Terminal-Bench 2.0, it gives researchers a common, reproducible way to benchmark agents at scale.
