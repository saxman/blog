[Edison](https://edisonscientific.com/)

The AI Platform for scientific R&D. Kosmos, the AI Scientist for R&D teams, takes you from hypothesis to registration in days, not months. It reads literature, analyzes data, generates hypotheses, steers investigations mid-run, branches hypotheses, and executes scientific workflows autonomously. It builds on your experimental history, decision patterns, and proprietary scientific data, embeds in existing workflows through Slack, Teams, and email, and remains model agnostic as new state-of-the-art models emerge. Predecessor research projects: ChemCrow (language agents for chemistry), PaperQA (deep research agent), and Aviary (trained AI agents for science).
