[humanlayer/12-factor-agents: What are the principles we can use to build LLM-powered software that is actually good enough to put in the hands of production customers?](https://github.com/humanlayer/12-factor-agents)

12-Factor Agents is an open guide by Dex Horthy (HumanLayer) that sets out twelve engineering principles for building reliable, production-grade LLM-powered software, modeled on the 12 Factor Apps methodology. It argues that effective agents are mostly deterministic code with LLM steps placed at key points. It recommends owning prompts, context windows, and control flow, and adding modular agent concepts to existing products instead of relying heavily on frameworks.
