[Paperclip](https://paperclip.gxl.ai/)

Paperclip transforms scientific knowledge into an agent-native virtual filesystem that AI agents can efficiently search, navigate, and synthesize. It indexes millions of full-text papers, clinical trials, regulatory documents, and biological databases, enabling agents to conduct much faster and more grounded deep synthesis.
