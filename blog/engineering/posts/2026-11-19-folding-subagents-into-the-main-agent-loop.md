[Building agents: When to fold subagents into your main agent loop](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-agents-when-to-fold-subagents-into-your-main-agent-loop)

This article from Anthropic's Applied AI team explains when enterprise teams should fold routers, planners, and specialist subagents into a single agent loop that uses skills, tool search, and progressive disclosure. It covers failure modes of org-chart-shaped multi-agent systems, a staged migration process driven by evals, and tools such as a migration kit and Claude API skills. It matters because a single agent with skills often matched or beat subagent designs on quality, at lower cost and latency.
