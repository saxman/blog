[Pro$^2$ Agent: Scientific Problem Probing for Autonomous AI R&D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25357)

Pro² Agent is an LLM-based agent for autonomous AI research. Instead of only iterating on code, it maintains an explicit "Problem State" that tracks the problems blocking success and the candidate solutions for them, and it updates this state through targeted research and controlled hypothesis testing. It matches the best reported medal rate on the full MLE-Bench under a 24-hour budget (70.7%) and reaches 76.0% with 48 hours, while producing reusable research notes that make its process transparent.
