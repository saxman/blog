[Orthomolecular medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthomolecular_medicine): "Orthomolecular medicine is a form of alternative medicine that claims to maintain human health through nutritional supplementation. It is rejected by evidence-based medicine. The concept builds on the idea of an optimal nutritional environment in the body and suggests that diseases reflect deficiencies in this environment."
