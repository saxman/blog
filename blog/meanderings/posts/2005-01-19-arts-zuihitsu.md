[Zuihitsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuihitsu): "Zuihitsu (随筆) is a genre of Japanese literature consisting of loosely connected personal essays and fragmented ideas that typically respond to the author's surroundings. The name is derived from two Kanji meaning "at will" and "pen.""

随筆 【ずいひつ】 (n) zuihitsu: essays; miscellaneous writings.
