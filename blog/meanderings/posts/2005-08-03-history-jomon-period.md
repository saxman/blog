[Jōmon period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C5%8Dmon_period): "In Japanese history, the Jōmon period is the time between c. 14,000 and 300 BC, during which Japan was inhabited by the Jōmon people, a diverse hunter-gatherer and early agriculturalist population united by a common culture, which reached a considerable degree of sedentism and cultural complexity."
