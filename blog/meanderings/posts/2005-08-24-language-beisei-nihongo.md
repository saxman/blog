米製日本語 "beisei nihongo": American-made Japanese.

Compare the reverse, [Wasei-eigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasei-eigo): "Wasei-eigo are Japanese-language expressions that are based on English words, or on parts of English phrases, but do not exist in standard English, or do not have the meanings that they have in standard English."
