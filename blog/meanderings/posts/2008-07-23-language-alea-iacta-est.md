[Alea iacta est](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alea_iacta_est): "Alea iacta est is a Latin phrase meaning "The die is cast". It is a variation of the words spoken by Julius Caesar at the crossing of the Rubicon on 10 January 49 BC. The phrase, referring to a game of chance played with dice, indicates that events have passed a point of no return and the outcome is uncertain."
