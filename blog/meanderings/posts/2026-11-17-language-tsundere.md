[Tsundere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsundere)

*Tsundere* is a Japanese term for a character archetype who is initially harsh, cold, or standoffish but gradually reveals a warmer, more affectionate side. The word combines the ideophones *tsun tsun* (aloof, standoffish) and *dere dere* (lovey-dovey, infatuated). It originated in Japanese *bishōjo* games and became part of the otaku *moe* phenomenon, spreading to anime, manga, maid cafés, and merchandise. Characters such as Lum from *Urusei Yatsura* and Madoka Ayukawa from *Kimagure Orange Road* have been cited as early examples.
